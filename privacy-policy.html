Bean Brawl Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Bean Brawl ("the game") is made by VOHRA APPS. This policy explains what information the game and its advertising partner handle, and the choices you have.

What the game itself stores

The game saves your progress on your device: scores, coins, heroes, gear, jelly beans, Pantry upgrades, Bean Pass progress, unlocked modes and your settings. If the game is closed in the middle of a run, that run is saved on your device so you can carry on. We do not run our own servers, and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.

Advertising

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. Some ads are optional videos you choose to watch for an in-game reward, such as continuing a run. To show, measure and protect ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares:

  • Your device's IP address, which may be used to estimate general location
  • Interactions with ads and the app, such as app launches, taps and video views
  • Diagnostic information, such as app launch time and performance
  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID

This data is used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, and is encrypted in transit. Google's use of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Game analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

To understand how the game is played and to fix bugs, the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics. They collect:

  • Game events, such as runs started and finished, scores, time survived, stage and difficulty, bosses beaten, cards and gear picked, rewards claimed and which ads were watched
  • Crash and error reports, including the device model, Android version, app version and the state of the game when the problem happened
  • A random app instance identifier and general device information, such as language and approximate country (from the IP address)

This data does not include your name, email address or contacts. It is used only for analytics and to fix crashes, is encrypted in transit, and is kept for up to 14 months. The app also uses Firebase Remote Config to receive game settings (such as festival event dates) without an update. Google handles this data under the Firebase privacy information and the Google Privacy Policy.

Leaderboards (Google Play Games)

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the game sends your high score and longest run to Google Play Games leaderboards. Other players can see your Play Games name, avatar and these scores. Google handles this data under the Google Privacy Policy. You can manage your Play Games profile and visibility in the Google Play Games app.

Cloud save: if you are signed in to Google Play Games, your game progress (such as coins, unlocked items, levels and settings) is saved to your Google Play Games account so you can continue on another device. It is stored by Google and is not shared with anyone else. You can delete it from the Google Play Games app or your Google Account settings.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases (such as Remove Ads, the Starter Pack, Hero Outfits and the Bean Pass) are handled by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details; the game only receives a confirmation from Google Play that a purchase was made, so it can unlock the item.

Notifications

If you allow it, the game can show a reminder on your phone (for example when your daily gift is ready). These reminders are scheduled on your device; no notification data is sent to us. You can turn them off in your Android settings.

Your choices

  • Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland: the game asks for your consent before personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time with the Privacy Options button in the game menu.
  • Personalised ads: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your Android device settings under Google > Ads (or Privacy > Ads).
  • Delete your game data: uninstall the game or clear its storage in Android settings. This removes everything the game saved on your device.

Children

Bean Brawl is intended for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
